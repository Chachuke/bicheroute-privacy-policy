# Privacy Policy — Bicheroute!

**Last updated: 16 August 2026**
**Applies to: Bicheroute! for iOS and Android (`com.chachuke.bicheroute`)**

## Summary

Bicheroute! is a point counter for a game played in a car. It works entirely
offline. It has no accounts, no servers, no advertising, and no third-party
analytics or tracking SDKs of any kind.

**We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.** Everything
you type into the app stays on your device, and nothing ever leaves it unless
you deliberately share it yourself using your phone's share sheet.

## Who we are

Bicheroute! is published by **Chachuke**, an independent developer.

For any question about this policy or about your data, contact us at
**<privacy@bicheroute.app>**.

Because the app collects no personal data and we operate no servers, we hold no
information about you and cannot identify any individual user.

## What the app stores on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, in its private application
storage:

| What | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Player names (first names or nicknames, up to 24 characters) | To display the scoreboard and the leaderboard | Local database on the device |
| Player colours and their order in the game | To tell players apart on screen | Local database on the device |
| Scoring events — points, distances read off a road sign, corrections, timestamps | To compute scores and rebuild past games | Local database on the device |
| Game history (games in progress and finished games) | To show the leaderboard across games | Local database on the device |
| One display setting: whether you dismissed the road-safety warning | So the app does not show it again | Local device preferences |

That is the complete list. The app does **not** access or store your location,
your contacts, your photos, your camera, your microphone, your address book,
your device identifiers, your advertising ID, or any diagnostic or usage
telemetry.

The names you enter are typically first names or nicknames of the people in the
car. They are personal data in the ordinary sense, but they are never sent
anywhere: they exist only in the app's private storage on your own device. You
are free to use initials or nicknames instead.

## What the app does not do

- **No account and no sign-in.** There is nothing to register.
- **No network access.** The released app requests no network permission on
  Android and makes no network requests on either platform.
- **No advertising.** There are no ads and no ad networks.
- **No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking.** The app embeds no
  third-party analytics, attribution, or advertising SDK. We receive no crash
  reports and no usage statistics.
- **No tracking across apps or websites**, as defined by Apple's App Tracking
  Transparency framework. The app never asks for tracking permission because it
  never tracks.
- **No sale or sharing of personal information**, as those terms are defined by
  the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

## Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions to play.

On iOS, the system may ask for permission to add to your photo library, and
only at one moment: if you choose **Save Image** in the share sheet after a
game. That permission lets the system write the recap image you asked to save.
The app never reads your photo library.

## Sharing an end-of-game recap

At the end of a game the app can turn the podium into an image. When you tap
Share, the app renders that image into your device's temporary folder and opens
your operating system's standard share sheet, prefilled with the image and a
short text naming the winner and their score.

Nothing is sent at that point. **You** choose the destination — a messaging app,
email, your photo library, or nothing at all. Once you send it, the content is
handled by the app or service you picked, under **its** privacy policy, not
ours. The temporary image file is written to the app's temporary folder and is
cleaned up by the operating system.

## Data retention and deletion

Your games stay on your device for as long as the app is installed. The app
keeps no copies elsewhere, and there is no backup to any server of ours.

The current version has no in-app "delete all data" button. To erase everything
the app has stored, **uninstall Bicheroute!** — both platforms delete the app's
private storage, including the game database and settings, when the app is
removed.

Note that if you have enabled your platform's own device backup (iCloud Backup
on iOS, Android Backup Service), your operating system may include app data in
those backups. Those backups are governed by Apple's and Google's privacy
policies, not by ours, and are managed in your device settings.

## Children

Bicheroute! is a family game and is suitable for children. It collects no
personal data, shows no advertising, and contains no third-party content, so it
does not knowingly collect any information from children of any age — because
it collects no information from anyone.

We recommend that a parent or guardian supervise which names are entered and
where a recap image is shared, as with any app that can send content to other
apps.

## Your rights

Under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California
Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and comparable laws, you have rights of
access, rectification, erasure, portability, and objection over your personal
data.

Because we never receive your data, we cannot access, export, correct, or
delete it on your behalf — we simply hold nothing. You exercise these rights
directly and immediately on your own device: edit or remove names in the app,
or uninstall the app to erase all of it at once.

If you consider that your rights have not been respected, you may lodge a
complaint with your local supervisory authority (in France, the CNIL —
<https://www.cnil.fr>).

## International transfers

to any third country.
